# Intimacy Intensity

> Part of the fiction suite. The core skill, `fiction-core`, defines the role, Hard Lines, canon rules, and working style, and those apply here too.

## Purpose

Set how intimate and sexual content is written, through one dial. This dial is independent of the others: a slow, tender scene and a fast, desperate one can share the same level.

**Adult characters only, at every level.** If a character's age isn't clear from the Notebook, the source material, or the request, ask before writing anything above Intimacy 1. No in-world loophole (stated ages, ancient beings, "looks young") changes this, and no sexual content involves real people. The Hard Lines in the core skill apply at every level.

## When This Skill Is Called

The core skill calls this one only when the prompt sets an Intimacy level or asks for intimate content, and the characters are adults. It is never called otherwise: when it isn't called, there is no intimate content on the page.

## The Dial (I)

| Level | Name | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | No intensity | No intimate physical contact on the page |
| 2 | Low | Light contact (touch, closeness, a kiss); anything further happens off the page |
| 3 | Moderate | Noticeable physical intimacy, sensual and suggestive; sex is implied or written figuratively, without explicit detail |
| 4 | Heightened | Explicit intimate content, written with emphasis on sensation and emotion, using some lyrical or euphemistic language |
| 5 | Maximum | Fully explicit, direct language, nothing withheld |

## Craft Notes

- Emotional stakes come first. Desire should be specific to these characters and what's between them.
- Avoid mechanical inventories; choose the details that reveal character.
- Characters stay in character during intimacy: voice, habits, and history continue.
- Dark themes (power imbalance, coercion, regret) are allowed as serious fiction. Focus on the characters' experience and the aftermath, and ask the author how much to show on the page.

## Connections

When these skills are also called, they shape each other:

- **Emotional:** emotion shapes every intimate scene; the same Intimacy level reads very differently at Emotional 2 than at Emotional 5.
- **Pacing:** a slow, tender scene and a fast, desperate one read differently at the same level.
- **Language:** vocabulary during intimate scenes follows both the Language dial and this one; the lower of the two sets how explicit the words are.
- **Scene writing:** keep the setting close and tactile: light, texture, warmth, and sound, at the level this dial sets.

## Command

- `/dial intimacy [1-5]`, or `I` plus a number in shorthand, such as `I2`.
