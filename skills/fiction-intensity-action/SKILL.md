# Action Intensity

> Part of the fiction suite. The core skill, `fiction-core`, defines the role, Hard Lines, canon rules, and working style, and those apply here too.

## Purpose

Set the physical intensity of a scene: fights, exertion, pain, and injury.

This dial is independent of the others. A brutal fight can be Action 5 with little emotion on the page, and a heartbreaking goodbye can be Action 1.

## When This Skill Is Called

The core skill calls this one when the prompt sets an Action level, or when the scene has a fight, chase, exertion, pain, or injury. When it isn't called, there is no deliberate physical intensity.

## The Dial (A)

| Level | Name | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | No intensity | No physical strain or conflict |
| 2 | Low | Light exertion or a brief scuffle; minor bruises or strain; kept quick |
| 3 | Moderate | A real fight or hard exertion on the page; injuries with consequences |
| 4 | Heightened | Brutal, sustained violence or exertion; serious injury, pain, and exhaustion in detail |
| 5 | Maximum | Extreme, relentless, life-or-death; graphic injury; nothing spared |

## Craft Notes

- **Clear geography:** where everyone is, what's in reach, what changes.
- **Cause and effect:** every blow, slip, and win has a reason and a cost.
- **The body keeps score:** fatigue, adrenaline, and injuries carry into later scenes, and canon tracks them.
- **Specific over graphic:** at 4-5, rely on precise details, not a wall of gore. One exact image hits harder than ten vague ones.
- **Narrative-level detail only:** real-world weapons and tactics stay at the level a published novel would use, never as working instructions.

## Connections

When these skills are also called, they shape each other:

- **Scene writing:** at Action 3 and above, make the space physical and readable: distances, footing, cover.
- **Pacing:** high Action with high Pacing is a blur of motion; high Action with low Pacing is slow, deliberate brutality.
- **Emotional:** fear, rage, and grief often rise with a fight; each still uses its own level.
- **Language:** characters under physical strain tend toward shorter, rawer speech, within the Language ceiling.

## Command

- `/dial action [1-5]`, or `A` plus a number in shorthand, such as `A4`.
